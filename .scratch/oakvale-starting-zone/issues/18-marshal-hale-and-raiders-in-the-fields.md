## Built

Built on 2026-09-28 by Claude in PR #36, in autonomous mode (Tom asked for the rest of Oakvale to run without his input).

- **The chain as data** (`src/quests.ts`): the three quests in order, each with its title, objectives, XP, any sword, and what Hale says while it's offered, under way and ready, plus Hale's line after the chain and "Return to Marshal Hale". A kill objective names its camp (the farm, the lumber camp) or its role (the Warden); a pickup objective names its item (the orders). The counts and XP are in `CONFIG.quests` (3 and 80, 5 and 120, 300).
- **The rules** (`src/adventureState.ts`): new events `accept`, `handIn` and `pickup` beside `kill`. New effects: `quest` (a stage reached: offered, active, ready, handed in), `progress` (an objective's new count) and `sword`. Each quest is _locked_, _offered_, _active_, _ready_ or _handed in_. The first is offered from the start, and each hand-in offers the next, so only one is ever under way and none goes back. A kill or pickup counts only for the quest under way, only on its own objectives, and never past the need; the quest is ready once every objective is done. The state answers `hale` (the marker, the board's line and its buttons), `tracker` (the title and a line per objective with its count, or "Return to Marshal Hale", or null) and `sword`.
- **Camp ids** are a `CampId` union (`farm`, `lumberCamp`, `patrol`, `watchtower`, `mine`) on `CampPlan.id` and the kill event, so a camp built under another name is a type error rather than a quest that never counts. Tickets 21, 22 and 27 must name their camps with these.
- **Marshal Hale** (`src/people/hale.ts`): the talk prototype's stand-in on today's rig, at the layout's new `hale` spot (the Zone's `hale`), facing the crossroads' centre. Within 8 m they turn to face you, and they ease back when you go. They wave when you come within 6 m, and again once you've been 10 m off. They're a 0.3 m body in the World (`World.addBody`), so nothing walks through them. The marker (`src/ui/questMarker.ts`) floats 0.5 m over their head: a gold "!", a grey "?" or a gold "?", bobbing, and nothing after the chain.
- **The talk board** (`src/ui/talkBoard.ts`): prototype A's parchment board with Hale's name, their line and chunky buttons. It unfolds within 2.3 m while you look within 50° of Hale's head, standing 0.6 m out from Hale towards you and 0.55 m to your right, turned to you. Either fist or the sword's tip presses, and that hand buzzes. It takes no press for 0.4 s after it unfolds or changes, or for 0.6 s after a press, and a probe already resting in a button must leave it first. It folds at 3.6 m, or when the talk ends, and then stays shut until you've been 3.6 m away.
- **The tracker** (`src/ui/questTracker.ts`): prototype C's, 1.1 m out towards the top left of your view, catching up with your head at a rate of 3 a second. It has the title in gold and a line per objective in white, flashes for 1.5 s on a quest taken, progress or ready, and hides while you have no quest.
- **The Adventure** wires them together. Kills go to the state as before, now also counting for the quest. "Accept" takes the quest and folds the board. "Hand in" gives it back: a fanfare (`sfx.fanfare`), "+80 XP" over Hale, "LEVEL 2" 0.6 s later above it with the War Cry's line in view, and the board then shows Hale's next offer. "Not now" and "Goodbye" fold it. The debug handle's `state` answers `hale` and `tracker`.
- **Tests:** `tests/questChain.test.ts` (29) at the adventure-state seam. They cover a new character's offer; Raiders in the Fields taken, counting, ready, a fourth kill not counting, and handed in for 80 XP and level 2; kill credit (not before it's taken, only its own camp, the patrol and the watchtower not counting, the Warden only once What Lies Below is taken, raised skeletons never); The Lumber Camp's orders only while it's under way and only once, and both of its objectives in either order; What Lies Below's Warden and its hand-in with the sword; the chain done, with no marker and nothing to take or hand in; one quest at a time, no hand-in before ready, none twice, each offered in turn; and the plain route: level 2 at 110 XP at the first hand-in, 3 at 370 at the second, 4 at 640 at the dig's brute, 910 after the Warden, and 5 with the sword at the last hand-in. `tests/world.test.ts` gained someone standing in the World being solid.
- **Checks:** `checks/hale.mjs` in headless Chromium with the emulator, all 34 passing. It covers the "!" over Hale at the start with Hale turned to you and waving, and the board unfolding on your right at 1.9 m with the offer and its two buttons. Standing on Hale's spot puts you 0.6 m off. `checks/adventure.mjs`, `checks/farm-camp.mjs`, `checks/levels.mjs` and `checks/world.mjs` still pass; the adventure check's walk now turns away from Hale first, since walking at them from the start now stops short.
