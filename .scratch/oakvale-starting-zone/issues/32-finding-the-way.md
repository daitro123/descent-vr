## Built

Built on 2026-09-30 by Claude, in autonomous mode (Tom asked for the rest of Oakvale to run without his input).

- **The quest arrow's target** is the adventure state's (`AdventureState.arrow`): each quest now names its place (`Quest.place`: the farm, the lumber camp, the mine), and while it's under way the arrow points there, beside its first objective not yet done; once it's ready, at Hale, beside "Return to Marshal Hale"; with no quest, nothing. The zone says where each place is (`Zone.places`, from the plan: the spot it points at and the clearing where it hides; the mine's points at its mouth and hides in its front).
- **The hide rule** (`src/ui/questArrow.ts`, pure): `arrowHides` (indoors or in the mine; in the place's clearing; within `CONFIG.tracker.arrow.nearHale`, 10 m, of Hale when it points at them), `arrowPoint` and `arrowTurn` (up is straight ahead, positive to the left).
- **On the tracker** (`QuestTracker.update(…, arrow)`): the arrow is a small card of its own (2.2 cm, gold edged in dark) hung on the tracker at the left of its line and turned about the tracker's face; the objective lines moved in to leave it room. It's a card like the tracker, so it's drawn with the tracker's shader and compiles nothing new. The Adventure works out the turn each frame from where your head is and which way it looks.
- **The signposts** (`SIGNPOSTS` in `layout.ts`, by the signpost's variant; `signpost()` in `buildings.ts`; the names in `wayfinding.ts` `buildSignNames`): the crossroads' five boards ("Old Mine" and "Lumber Camp" north, "Farm" east, "Pond" west, "Brackenmoor" south), 0.25 m apart from 2.3 m down to 1.3 m. The second, smaller one (a 2.3 m post, 0.85 m boards) stands west of the main road at (−10, −45.5), between where the watchtower's road leaves it (east) and the lumber camp's (west): "Old Mine", "Lumber Camp", "Watchtower", "Village". Each signpost's names are one texture (a row per board, pale paint on the plank) on one mesh: a quad on both faces of each board, each reading left to right, 20 and 16 triangles.
- **The map board** (`MAP_BOARD`, structure `mapboard` at (6.3, 4.4), yaw facing west; frame, posts and a little slate roof in `buildings.ts`; the face in `wayfinding.ts` `buildMapFace`): 1.2 × 0.9 m, its top at 1.5 m, leaning back 10°, 2.5 m east of the signpost on the farm road's south side, across the signpost from Hale. The face is one 1024 × 768 texture painted once from the plan: the land shaded by height, a dab per tree, the fields, the stream, the pond, the roads, the bridge and dock, every building, the watchtower, the standing stones and the mine's mouth; "Oakvale" and a north arrow, the places named (Inn, Smithy, Farm, Lumber Camp, Watchtower, Standing Stones, Old Mine, Pond), "To Brackenmoor" with an arrow at the south road, and a red "You are here" where the board stands. 98 triangles in all (the frame's 96 go into the zone's chunk; the face is one quad), two draw calls at most.
- **Smoke** (`src/world/smoke.ts` `Smoke`, pure `puffAt`; the plumes from the plan, `ForestLayout.smoke`): one instanced mesh of 60 soft puffs (10 per plume) from the inn's two chimneys, the house by the well's, the plain cottage's (the thatched one has no chimney), the smithy's forge flue and the lumber camp's fire; not the farmhouse. Each puff faces you, rises 8 m (the fire's 5 m, smaller and darker) over 9 s, widens, drifts on a steady north-easterly breeze and thins away, its opacity a per-puff attribute. The chimneys' places moved into the plans so the smoke and the buildings share them (`INN.chimneys`, `HOUSE.chimney.rise`, `COTTAGE_CHIMNEY`, `SMITHY.flue`).
- **Shaders**: the Adventure compiles the zone's meshes as it starts, so the smoke (behind you at the start) and the signs' and the map's painted material (after a load in the mine) never compile mid-walk.
- **Tunables**: `CONFIG.tracker.arrow` (its size and the 10 m round Hale) and `CONFIG.smoke` (puffs, life, opacity, rise, size, drift, sway, breeze, colours). The boards' and the map board's sizes are the plan's, beside the signposts (`SIGNPOSTS`, `MAP_BOARD`), as the tent's and the buildings' are.
- **Tests**: `tests/questChain.test.ts` (the arrow's target and line for every stage of the chain, the orders taken first, across a reload); `tests/questArrow.test.ts` (the hide rule, where it points, how it turns); `tests/forest.test.ts` (the crossroads signpost's names and ways; the second signpost north of the bridge between the two forks, off every road, its boards along their roads; the boards clear of each other; the map board 2.5 m east of the signpost, south of the farm road, facing west, off the roads, its size and the zone it shows; each quest's place is its camp's; the smoke's six plumes); `tests/world.test.ts` (the names one mesh a signpost, the map board under 100 triangles, the smoke one instanced mesh of 60).
- **Checks**: `checks/finding-the-way.mjs` (see its header). Screenshots of both signposts and the map board from a few steps away, at about the Quest 3's pixels per degree, and the smoke, are in the project's files under `finding-the-way/`.

Calls **taken on Tom's behalf**, to revisit:

- **The map is painted heads-up**: facing the board you face east, so east is at its top and north at its left, as the zone lies before you, with a north arrow. A "you are here" map lined up with the world is the easier one to read; a north-up map would have the mine at the top and you'd turn it in your head.
- **The arrow hides within 10 m of Hale only when it points at them.** Setting out from the crossroads for the farm, it shows from the start.
- **The farm's and the lumber camp's arrows point at their clearings' middles**, and hide in the same clearings the camps use; the mine's points at its mouth and hides from its front on, as the spec says.
- **The second signpost stands west of the main road**, 1.8 m off its edge between the two forks, where there's room clear of all three roads; its boards point along each road as it leaves (the watchtower's east-north-east, the mine's north-north-west), not due north, east, south and west.
- **The crossroads' boards were re-spaced** 0.25 m apart for the fifth ("Lumber Camp" under "Old Mine"), so the lowest ("Brackenmoor") is at 1.3 m.
- **The names are pale paint on the plank**, on both faces of each board, so you read them coming from either side.
- **The map board has a little slate roof**, like the well's, and names the inn and the smithy too.
- **The smoke** is mid-grey (the fire's darker), at most 0.55 opaque, 0.8 m to 3 m across, rising 8 m over 9 s on a steady breeze: tuned in headless screenshots so the plumes read over the haze from the watchtower road. Numbers to tune on the headset: `CONFIG.smoke`.
