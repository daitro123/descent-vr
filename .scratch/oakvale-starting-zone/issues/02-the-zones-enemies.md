# The zone's enemies

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 

## Question

Which humanoid enemies live in Oakvale, where do they live, and what does each one test in the player?

- Candidates: kobold-style miners in the mine, bandits at the lumber camp, the undead deeper in the mine.
- The four existing behaviours can wear new looks: the grunt (directional melee), the archer (ranged), the brute (armour and a slam) and the Warden (a boss). Which to reuse, and whether any new behaviour is needed.
- How difficulty climbs across the zone's first few levels.
- Whether the zone has a boss, for example at the end of the quest chain in the mine.

## Answer

Settled with Tom over three rounds on 2026-09-28; the rounds are under Comments.

- **Two families, no new behaviours.** **Bandits** live outside and are new, on the human body from [Friendly characters](11-friendly-characters.md): the thug (grunt behaviour), the archer, and the leader (brute behaviour). **The undead** live in the mine and are today's skeletons as they are, on all four behaviours. No kobold-style miners.
- **Where they live**, as starting counts that [Enemies in the open](07-enemies-in-the-open.md) checks against the budget:
  - The farm: 4 thugs in two pairs. Raiders in the Fields needs 3.
  - The lumber camp: 4 thugs, 2 archers, and the leader by their tent. The Lumber Camp needs 5 of the 7, and the leader counts.
  - The watchtower: a lookout of 2 thugs and 1 archer, which no quest sends you to.
  - The old mine: the undead only. Skeleton grunts and archers near the mouth, brutes deeper, the Warden at the bottom. The bandits who dug there are already dead, so bandits and the undead never share a fight. Counts and placement belong to [The mine inside](09-the-mine-inside.md).
  - The village, the bridge, the pond and the standing stones are safe. Road patrols are Enemies in the open's call.
- **What each place tests, and how it climbs.** Each place adds one test in chain order: the farm teaches reading and blocking a swing, the lumber camp adds ranged pressure and the leader's unblockable slam, the mine puts every undead behaviour together, then the Warden. Each place also has an enemy level that sets health and damage, rising from the farm, to the lumber camp and the watchtower, to the mine, with the Warden highest. The numbers belong to [Progression, death and saving](03-progression-death-and-saving.md).
- **One boss: the Warden**, as it is today (the three-hit combo, the slam, skeletons raised at 70% and 40% health), cast as the long-dead thing the bandits' digging woke. It rises only while What Lies Below is active; before that, its chamber holds an empty throne. Dying or fleeing resets it to full health, and the skeletons it raised crumble when it falls or resets. Once beaten it stays dead. Its health (1100, tuned for the arena's last wave) is retuned for the zone.
- **Refilling:** every camp refills after it is cleared, the mine's undead included, before and after the chain. Only the Warden stays dead. The leader's orders don't come back once handed in.
- **No names or levels over heads** in Oakvale: you read an enemy by its look. Showing them can come back with quest tracking if levels prove hard to judge on the headset.
- **Looks:** the bandits are dressed in Friendly characters, alongside Marshal Hale. Enemies in the open prototypes its camp with skeletons standing in.
