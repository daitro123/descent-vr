# The zone's enemies

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked by: 

## Question

Which humanoid enemies live in Oakvale, where do they live, and what does each one test in the player?

- Candidates: kobold-style miners in the mine, bandits at the lumber camp, the undead deeper in the mine.
- The four existing behaviours can wear new looks: the grunt (directional melee), the archer (ranged), the brute (armour and a slam) and the Warden (a boss). Which to reuse, and whether any new behaviour is needed.
- How difficulty climbs across the zone's first few levels.
- Whether the zone has a boss, for example at the end of the quest chain in the mine.

## Comments

**2026-09-28:** [The quest chain](01-the-quest-chain.md) is resolved. Its story puts bandits outside and the undead in the mine: bandits raid the farm and hold the lumber camp (with a leader whose tent is there), and the chain ends by defeating whatever woke the dead at the bottom of the mine, which stays dead once beaten. Which enemies those are, and whether the thing at the bottom is the Warden, is still this ticket's call.

**2026-09-28, round 1 (Tom took every recommendation):**

- Families: two. **Bandits** outside, new, on the human body from [Friendly characters](11-friendly-characters.md). **The undead** in the mine, which are today's skeletons as they are. No kobold-style miners: the bandits do the digging in the story, and a third family would need a third body.
- Behaviours: no new one. Bandits wear the grunt, the archer and the brute; the undead keep all four. New behaviours wait until after Oakvale.
- The bottom of the mine: the Warden as it is today (crowned skeleton, three-hit combo, slam, raises skeletons at 70% and 40% health), cast as the long-dead thing the bandits' digging woke. It is Oakvale's one boss. Its health (1100) was tuned as the arena's last wave, so it is retuned for the zone later.
- The bandit leader: at the lumber camp by their tent, on the brute behaviour, and counts as one of quest 2's five. It gives the camp a climax and teaches the unblockable slam before the mine.
- Places off the chain: a small bandit lookout at the watchtower, a fight no quest sends you to. The village, the bridge, the pond and the standing stones stay safe. Whether anything patrols the roads stays with [Enemies in the open](07-enemies-in-the-open.md).

**2026-09-28, round 2 (Tom took every recommendation):**

- Outdoor rosters, as starting counts for [Enemies in the open](07-enemies-in-the-open.md) to check against the budget:
  - The farm: 4 bandit thugs (grunt behaviour) in two pairs. Quest 1 needs 3.
  - The lumber camp: 4 thugs, 2 archers and the leader. Quest 2 needs 5 of the 7.
  - The watchtower: 2 thugs and 1 archer.
- The mine holds the undead only: skeleton grunts and archers near the mouth, brutes deeper, the Warden at the bottom. The bandits who dug there are already dead, so bandits and the undead never share a fight. Counts and placement stay with [The mine inside](09-the-mine-inside.md).
- Difficulty climbs two ways. Each place adds one new test in chain order: the farm is thugs only (read and block a swing), the lumber camp adds archers and the leader's slam, the mine puts every undead behaviour together, then the Warden. And each place has an enemy level that sets health and damage, rising from the farm, to the lumber camp and the watchtower, to the mine, with the Warden highest. The numbers belong to [Progression, death and saving](03-progression-death-and-saving.md).
- The bandit looks (thug, archer, leader) are folded into [Friendly characters](11-friendly-characters.md), which builds the human body once and dresses it for Marshal Hale and the bandits.
- [Enemies in the open](07-enemies-in-the-open.md) prototypes its lumber camp with skeletons standing in for bandits, so it doesn't wait on the human body.
