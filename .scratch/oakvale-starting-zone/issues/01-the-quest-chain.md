# The quest chain

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked by: 

## Question

Which two or three quests does Oakvale's quest giver hand out, and how do they lead a new player through the zone?

- For each quest: where it sends you (the mine, the lumber camp, the farm, the watchtower), what it asks (defeat, collect, find, deliver), how it ends, and what it pays.
- Who gives them, and where that person stands (the inn, the crossroads).
- Whether they form a chain that ends in the mine, or sit side by side.

## Comments

**2026-09-28, round 1 (Tom took every recommendation):**

- Shape: a chain of three that ends in the mine: the farm, then the lumber camp, then the mine.
- Threat: bandits in the woods and the undead in the mine. The bandits dug into the mine for silver and woke the dead. This picks the story only; the roster is The zone's enemies' call.
- Quest giver: a marshal (the village guard captain) standing outdoors at the crossroads by the signpost. They give all three quests, so the chain doesn't wait on an interior.
- Objectives: mostly defeat, plus one find where the player picks an object up by hand. No deliver (it needs a second NPC) and no collect (it needs drops and an inventory).
- Hand-ins and rewards: walk back to the marshal to hand in each quest. Every quest pays XP; the finale also pays the item. Amounts and the item itself belong to Progression, death and saving.

**2026-09-28, round 2 (Tom took every recommendation):**

- Quest 1, the farm: bandits are raiding the farm. Defeat 3 of them in the fields, then hand in at the marshal.
- Quest 2, the lumber camp: the bandits have taken the lumber camp across the bridge. Defeat 5 of them and take the bandit leader's orders from his tent by hand (the find). The orders say the gang is digging for silver in the old mine.
- Quest 3, the mine: the marshal sends you into the old mine, where the bandits dug too deep and woke the dead. Fight down to whatever woke them, defeat it, and report back for the item. Its form and the mine's layout belong to The zone's enemies and The mine inside.
- Gating: none. The zone is open, enemies live in their places whether or not you have the quest, and kills and pickups count only while the quest is active.
- After the chain: the marshal's last line points you south through the pass toward the next zone. No quest there.
- Names (placeholders): Marshal Hale; "Raiders in the Fields", "The Lumber Camp", "What Lies Below".
