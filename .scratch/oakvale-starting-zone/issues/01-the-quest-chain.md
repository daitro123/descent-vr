# The quest chain

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 

## Question

Which two or three quests does Oakvale's quest giver hand out, and how do they lead a new player through the zone?

- For each quest: where it sends you (the mine, the lumber camp, the farm, the watchtower), what it asks (defeat, collect, find, deliver), how it ends, and what it pays.
- Who gives them, and where that person stands (the inn, the crossroads).
- Whether they form a chain that ends in the mine, or sit side by side.

## Answer

Settled with Tom over three rounds on 2026-09-28; the rounds are under Comments.

- **One quest giver, Marshal Hale** (the village guard captain), stands outdoors at the crossroads by the signpost. They give all three quests and take each one back, so no interior is on the chain's path.
- **A chain of three**, each unlocked by handing in the one before. Only one quest is active at a time; quests can't be dropped or repeated.
  1. **Raiders in the Fields** (the farm, south of the stream): bandits are raiding the farm. Defeat 3 of them there.
  2. **The Lumber Camp** (across the bridge): the bandits have taken the camp. Defeat 5 of them there, and take the bandit leader's orders from their tent by hand, in either order. The orders say the gang is digging for silver in the old mine.
  3. **What Lies Below** (the old mine): the bandits dug too deep and woke the dead. Fight down to whatever woke them and defeat it.
- **Story:** bandits in the woods, the undead in the mine. The roster and the thing at the bottom of the mine belong to The zone's enemies; the mine's layout to The mine inside.
- **Objectives** are defeat (only enemies at the quest's own place count) plus one find, a pickup by hand. No deliver, no collect.
- **Hand-ins and rewards:** walk back to the marshal to hand in each quest. Every quest pays XP; the last also pays the item. Amounts and the item belong to Progression, death and saving.
- **No gating:** the zone is open from the start. Enemies live in their places whether or not you have the quest, and kills and pickups count only while it is active.
- **After a hand-in** the farm and the lumber camp refill like any camp (timing in Enemies in the open). The thing at the bottom of the mine stays dead once beaten.
- **After the chain** the marshal's last line points you south through the pass toward the next zone. There is no quest there.
- Names are placeholders, cheap to change in the spec.
