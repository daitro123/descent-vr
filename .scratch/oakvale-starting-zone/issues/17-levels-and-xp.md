# 17: Levels and XP

**What to build:** Kills pay XP, and you climb from level 1 towards 5. Each level adds 20 health and 20% damage, and an enemy of a level takes the same step. "+10 XP" floats where a bandit falls; "LEVEL 2" plays with a sound and names what it unlocked; the belt shows your level and a thin XP bar. The War Cry and rage wait for level 2 and the ground slam for level 3. This ticket starts the **adventure state**, the pure module of progress the rest of the chain builds on.

**Spec:** Implementation Decisions › The adventure state (numbers and unlocks), Enemies, The player, Talking and tracking (floating text, the belt). User stories 74–84.

**Blocked by:** 16 (The farm's camp).

**Status:** done

- [x] A new adventure state module with no three.js in it: events in, effects and answers out. This ticket gives it level, XP and XP to the next level; maximum health; the damage multiplier; the unlocked abilities; and the kill event (the enemy's level and role: ordinary, leader, deep brute, the Warden, or raised by the Warden), whose effects are the XP gained and any levels reached with what they unlock.
- [x] The numbers: maximum health 100 + 20 per level above 1; damage × (1 + 0.2 per level above 1); levels at 100, 300, 600 and 1,000 XP in all; XP past 1,000 is dropped; a kill pays 10 XP per enemy level, triple for the leader, the deep brutes and the Warden, and nothing for skeletons the Warden raises.
- [x] Making an enemy takes its behaviour, its level and whether it's in a camp: health and damage × (1 + 0.2 per level above 1), then × 1.4 in a camp. The arena builds its enemies through the same code at level 1 with no camp multiplier, so it plays as today. The farm's camp is level 1.
- [x] The player's maximum health and damage multiplier come from the adventure state. The War Cry needs level 2 and Earthshaker level 3; before that their inputs do nothing, and rage builds only once the War Cry is unlocked. In the arena everything is unlocked at level 1, as today.

- [x] The Adventure's belt shows the level number and a thin XP bar between the health and rage orbs. The rage orb and each ability's pips appear once unlocked; the dash cooldown stays. The arena's belt is unchanged.
- [x] Tests at the adventure-state seam: health and damage per level; enemies' numbers per level, in and out of camps; kill XP by role; the cap dropping XP; unlocks at 2 and 3; a level-up's effects naming its unlocks.
- [x] Checked in headless Chromium: killing the farm's camp at level 1 pays 40 XP and shows the floats.
- [x] Every new number is in the game's table of tunables.

## Built

Built on 2026-09-28 by Claude in PR #35, in autonomous mode (Tom asked for the rest of Oakvale to run without his input).

- **The adventure state** (`src/adventureState.ts`, no three.js): `AdventureState` answers `level`, `xp` (in all), `xpToNext`, `progress` (0 to 1 through the level, for the belt) and `stats` (maximum health, damage multiplier, abilities). `apply(event)` takes a kill (`{ kind: 'kill', camp, level, role }`, the camp's id or null, the enemy's own level, and its role: ordinary, leader, deepBrute, warden or raised) and returns its effects: `{ kind: 'xp', amount }` for the XP actually kept, then one `{ kind: 'level', level, unlocks }` per level reached. `statsAt(level)` and `enemyNumbers(def, level, inCamp)` are its pure number rules; `ABILITIES` lists every ability. Every number is in `CONFIG.levels`: the XP totals `[100, 300, 600, 1000]`, 20 health and a 0.2 step per level, 10 XP per enemy level times the role's multiple (1, 3, 3, 3, 0), unlocks (War Cry 2, Earthshaker 3), and how long the floats and banners show.
- **Enemies by level** (`src/enemies/kinds.ts`): `createEnemy(kind, x, z, { level, inCamp, variant, def })` makes an enemy with `enemyNumbers`: health and every blow's damage × (1 + 0.2 per level above 1), × 1.4 in a camp, rounded once. The enemy keeps its `level`. Camps raise their members at the camp's level, in a camp; the arena makes its own at level 1 in none, so a wave-1 grunt still has 45 health and the duelist is unchanged. `campStrength` is gone. `PostPlan` gained an optional `role` (leader or deepBrute) for the lumber camp and the mine.
- **The player** (`src/player/player.ts`): `stats` holds your level's numbers (the arena's default is level 1 with every ability); `maxHp` reads from it, `can(ability)` asks it, and `addRage` does nothing until the War Cry has come. Combat (`src/combat/combat.ts`) multiplies every blow you deal by your level's damage (sword, shield bash, Earthshaker, War Cry, reflected arrows; the frenzy still multiplies on top), and the War Cry and Earthshaker do nothing until your level brings them.
- **The Adventure** (`src/adventure.ts`) owns the adventure state and feeds it each camp kill from `onKill`. "+N XP" floats in gold 1.9 m over where the enemy fell. A level-up sets your new numbers, fills your health, plays `sfx.levelUp` (a rising four-note call over a held chime) and shows "LEVEL N" in gold 2 m ahead with a line under it per ability it brought. The debug handle has `state`.
- **The belt** (`src/ui/beltHud.ts`): in the Adventure, "LV2" in the belt's pixel font (new L and V glyphs) over a one-pixel gold XP bar, above the dash bar. The rage orb and each ability's pip draw only once unlocked, so the arena, where everything is unlocked, looks as it did.
- **Tests:** `tests/adventureState.test.ts` (17) at the adventure-state seam: a new character; kill XP by role and nothing for the raised; the levels at 100, 300, 600 and 1,000 with health and damage at each; progress through a level; a level-up's effect and the XP to the next; unlocks at 2 and 3 and nothing at 4 and 5; two levels in one kill; the cap keeping only what fits and dropping the rest; and enemies' numbers at level 1 (the arena's, unchanged), per level, in camps, and everything else unchanged. `tests/camps.test.ts` gained a level-2 camp (76 health for a thug, and its members at level 2).
- **Checks:** `checks/levels.mjs` in headless Chromium with the emulator, all 32 passing. A new character is level 1 with 100 health; the belt shows the level and an empty XP bar with no rage orb or pips; rage doesn't build, and A, X and the ground slam do nothing. Killing the farm's camp at level 1 pays 40 XP, with a gold "+10 XP" over each of the four where it fell, and the XP bar fills to 8 of 20. From the village the farm refills; the tenth kill reaches level 2 at 100 XP with "LEVEL 2" and "War Cry: press A or X", health filled to 120, the rage orb and the War Cry's pip on the belt, rage building and A letting out the War Cry, while the slam still waits. The thirtieth kill reaches level 3 at 320 XP with the Earthshaker's line and 140 health, and the slam fires. The arena still has 100 health, plain damage, every ability and a 45-health wave-1 grunt. `checks/adventure.mjs` (its belt check now looks for the level and no enemies left), `checks/farm-camp.mjs` and `checks/world.mjs` still pass. Calls **taken on Tom's behalf**, to revisit:

- **The level step applies to every blow you deal**: the sword, the shield bash, Earthshaker, the War Cry's blast and reflected arrows, since the spec says "your damage".
- **At the cap, a kill floats only the XP kept** ("+10 XP" for a 20 XP kill at 990), and nothing once you're at 1,000.
- **A kill that passes two levels at once** (not possible on the plain route) shows one "LEVEL N", the higher, with a line for every ability both levels brought.
- **A level-up while you're down doesn't lift you**: it fills your health only if you're standing.
- **The belt reads "LV2"**, not a bare number, in its pixel font, over a one-pixel gold XP bar (full at the cap). The rage orb and pips are hidden, not greyed, until they come.
- **How it shows:** "+N XP" a little larger than a damage number, for 1.6 s; "LEVEL N" for 3.5 s and its lines for 5 s, fixed where you were looking, like "YOU DIED". The level-up sound is a new synthesised call, distinct from the arena's victory sting.
- **Each enemy knows its own level** and the kill pays by it, not by its camp's, so the mine's camp can mix level 3 and 4 members when ticket 25 to 27 give posts their own levels. Every camp today is one level.
- **Enemy numbers round once** after both steps: a level-2 camp's thug has 76 health (45 × 1.2 × 1.4 = 75.6).
