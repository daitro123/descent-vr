# 34: Oakvale in streamed chunks

**What to build:** Oakvale stops being built whole. A zone becomes a pure plan plus a chunk builder, and a streamer keeps the chunks near you at full detail, the ones farther out as cheap stand-ins, and the ones past the fog unloaded. Walking the length of Oakvale looks as it does today, while `?perf` shows chunks changing detail as you go. This is the prefactor for a second zone.

**Spec:** Implementation Decisions › Zones: a plan and chunks, The streamer. User stories 125 (Oakvale's part) and 143 (loaded chunks).

**Blocked by:** 24 (The house by the well and the smithy), 26 (The old mine: down to the Warden's hall).

**Status:** done

- [x] A zone is a pure plan plus a chunk builder, both free of the DOM so they run in a worker and in tests. It registers an id, its display name, its origin on the grid and its neighbours. `GameMap` and `MapInfo` change shape to this; the registry still finds zones by folder. Oakvale keeps its id, `forest`, and is labelled "Oakvale". The crypt stays a whole-build map for the viewer and `?map=crypt`, and the arena keeps building its own hall.

## Built

Built on 2026-09-30 by Claude, in autonomous mode (Tom asked for the rest of Oakvale to run without his input).

- **The grid** (`src/world/chunks.ts`): one 40 m grid over every zone, chunks centred on multiples of 40 and keyed `"i,j"` in world metres (`chunkKey`, `chunkAt`, `chunkBounds`, `chunkDistance` to a chunk's nearest point). A chunk comes back as `ChunkData`: plain `Float32Array`s (position, normal, colour, the model material's `fx` and uv) and a bounding sphere, ready to transfer from a worker in 35. `ChunkSource` is a zone's keys and its builder.
- **The zone's shape** (`src/maps/types.ts`): `MapInfo` is a `ZoneInfo` (`kind: 'zone'`, id, `label`, `origin` on the grid, `neighbours`, `load`) or a `WholeInfo` (`kind: 'whole'`, the crypt, still built whole for the viewer and `?map=crypt`). A `Zone` now carries its `chunks`, its `walkable` shape and its `seams`; its `root` holds only the extras. Oakvale keeps the id `forest`, is labelled "Oakvale", sits at origin (0, 0) and has no neighbours yet. The arena builds its hall as before.
- **The plan** (`buildLayout`, pure) now also holds the atmosphere (`OAKVALE_ATMOSPHERE`, moved from forest.ts), the walkable shape and the crest's seam profile (the ground's height every 2 m, its terrain grid, along z = 140 from x −100 to 100, for 36).
- **Oakvale's chunk builder** (`src/maps/forest/chunks.ts` `buildOakvaleChunk`, DOM-free): everything in the plan goes in the chunk its origin is in (a tree, a building, a field, a fence's first post, a road's stretch; anything past the terrain's edge in the nearest of the 7 × 7 chunks). The ground, roads, worn patches and plants are hashed per element, not drawn from one running random sequence, so a chunk comes out byte for byte the same whatever was built before it. Full detail adds up to exactly the old whole build (264,304 triangles). A **stand-in** is coarse ground (a height every 4 m, the roads coloured in as dirt) with a skirt hung 0.4 m below its edge so no crack shows against a full neighbour, the mountains' cheaper far trees, the buildings and fields as they are, and no undergrowth, fences or worn patches: about 52% of full overall, and less than full in every chunk. Buildings, fields and fences are modelled once per plan and stamped again when a chunk changes detail.
- **The extras** (`buildForest`): the glows, the windmill's sails, water, smoke, the signposts' names and the map board, built once on the main thread; the glows and sails come from running the structure builders with recording callbacks, so their places stay the buildings' own. Water moved to `src/maps/forest/water.ts`.
- **The decision** (`src/world/streaming.ts`, pure): `decide(x, z, keys, now, reach)` gives each chunk full detail within 120 m of its nearest point, a stand-in out to the fog's far edge (200 m in Oakvale, or as far as you see with the viewer's fog lifted), and nothing beyond; one chunk (40 m) of hysteresis on each radius: full is kept to 160 m, a stand-in fetched at the fog's edge plus 40 m and dropped past it plus 80 m. Full is never farther out than the fog's edge. `beyondFog` culls a chunk whose sphere lies wholly past the fog's far edge.
- **The streamer** (`src/world/streamer.ts`): the World's, streaming every loaded zone's chunks into a group of their own beside the zone's extras. While you walk it builds at most one chunk a frame (`CONFIG.streaming.perFrame`), nearest first, stages it (uploaded unseen for a render) and swaps it in the next frame; dropping a chunk is free. It fills in everything wanted at once on loading in, on waking after a death, and when the viewer jumps to a spot or toggles the fog. It pauses while the outdoors is hidden.
- **Staging** (`src/world/staging.ts` `Stager`): round the next render alone (the scene's `onBeforeRender` and `onAfterRender`), a staged mesh that isn't showing is shown in a material that never draws, and every staged mesh is made uncullable, so the renderer uploads its vertices without drawing anything new; after the render everything is put back. Hidden lights are never shown. The inn's, the house's and the mine's meshes are staged the first time you come within 40 m of the door or mouth (`CONFIG.interiors.stage`); with the mine go its undead and the Warden, with the inn its innkeeper (who hangs from its room), and with every fill the zone's extras, its chunks and the outdoors' villagers (`World.stageWith`).
- **The walkable area** (`src/maps/walkable.ts` `Walkable`): a union of convex polygons, their shared edges removed, which keeps a body its radius inside. Oakvale's is its ±84 m square as one polygon; the colliders take it in place of the bounds clamp, and the World picks the zone underfoot by it.
- **`?perf`** adds a line `chunks     N full M far`.
- **Fixed on the way**: `?map=forest` could start in the mine's dark light for a few seconds: a first frame's time from before its timer started ran the mine's light backwards past 1. The World now never steps backwards.
- **Tunables**: `CONFIG.streaming` (`chunk` 40, `full` 120, `hysteresis` 40, `perFrame` 1, `standIn.cell` 4 and `.skirt` 0.4) and `CONFIG.interiors.stage` 40.
- **Tests**: `tests/streaming.test.ts` (the grid; the registry's zone and whole map; the crest profile; chunk builds byte for byte deterministic in any order; full detail adding up to the old total; stand-ins cheaper everywhere; spheres holding every vertex; the skirt; the decisions at the crossroads, the mine front and a corner, and walking south and back through the hysteresis; the fog cull; the streamer's fill, one build a frame nearest first, staging then swapping, dropping, culling, pausing; the stager; the walkable shape) and `tests/world.test.ts` (the room staged at exactly 40 m round its render; the mine's undead staged with it and the outdoors with each fill; chunks hidden with the outdoors). The forest layout tests pass unchanged.
- **Checks**: `checks/streaming.mjs` (see its header), against this build and main before it. `?fly=forest` from all eleven of the viewer's spots, `?map=forest` from its start and the Adventure from its start look as before (pixels differ by 0.2 to 3.0 of 255 on average; the inn's 3.0 is its hearth's flicker, the same room and props), with no more shader programs (7, 7 and 18). Walking the main road from the mine's front to the southern edge looking south, one chunk at most is built in a frame (24 over 98 steps), and the frame draws 301.5k triangles on average against 305.8k before, from 476k at the mine's front down to 160k near the edge, never above before in two runs of three; in the other, five steps round z 40 drew a little over (the margin wasn't recorded, and the patrol and the villagers' work move between runs). `?perf` shows `chunks     40 full 9 far`. At `?map=forest` the mine's rock, flagstones and bricks aren't uploaded 45 m from its mouth and all of them are at 38 m, still outside. And `?map=forest` starts in the daylight. Screenshots under `streamed-chunks/` in the project's files (each view next to its `-before`).

Calls **taken on Tom's behalf**, to revisit:

- **The radii are measured to a chunk's nearest point**, not its centre, so a chunk you stand beside is always full.
- **The hysteresis at the far radius**: a stand-in is fetched a chunk past the fog's far edge (240 m) and dropped a chunk later (280 m), so what the fog lets you glimpse is always there and a chunk at the edge doesn't flicker in and out. Full detail is kept to 160 m once built.
- **What a stand-in keeps**: buildings and fields as they are (they're the silhouettes you navigate by, and cheap next to the trees), the mountains' far trees, the ground at 4 m with the roads coloured in; undergrowth, fences and worn patches go (too small to see past 120 m).
- **Extras stay whole**: the glows, sails, water, smoke, signposts' names and map board are built once for the zone (few triangles, and each has its own material), not per chunk.
- **Chunks live in a group the World owns** beside the zone's root, so two Worlds (the tests) don't stream into the same zone.
- **The streamer pauses while the outdoors is hidden** (inside with the door shut, or past the mine's bend), and catches up a chunk a frame when you come out; the fill on waking and at load builds everything at once behind the fade.
- **The perf line** reads `chunks     40 full 9 far`.
- **Oakvale's neighbours are empty** until Brackenmoor comes in 36, and the crest's profile is recorded now so 36 can match it.
