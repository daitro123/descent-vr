# Item UI in VR

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

## Question

How should a player pick up, inspect, compare and equip one of about 3 items in VR, without breaking the flow of combat?

Candidate interactions to prototype and try on the headset:

- A physical belt or backpack you reach over your shoulder to stow and draw from.
- Grabbing an item from the floor and looking at a floating stat card to inspect it.
- A wrist menu (turn your left wrist to see a small panel).
- Equipment shown on the body: the sword or shield visibly changes.

Build a throwaway prototype of one or two of these with placeholder items, try it on the headset, and pick one. Loot depth is deliberately light, so the question is how it feels to handle items, not their stats.
