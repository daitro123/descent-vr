# Floor layout and the descent

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 04

## Question

What do the slice's hand-built floors look like, and how does the player physically go down between them?

- The size and shape of each floor: an arena, a few rooms plus corridors, or a single winding path?
- How enemies are placed and triggered: waves in rooms, ambushes, or roaming groups?
- The descent itself: stairs you walk, a hole you drop through, or a lift or cage. Which is comfortable in VR and still feels like *descending*?
- Enemy navigation: is steering around colliders enough, or does the slice need a navmesh?

Constrained by how a run works (floor count and length) and by the Quest 3 performance budget (room size, lights, enemy count).
