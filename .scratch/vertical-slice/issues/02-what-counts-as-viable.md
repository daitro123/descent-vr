# What counts as viable?

Type: grilling
Status: claimed
Blocked by:

## Question

After playing the finished slice on a Quest 3, what concrete signals decide "keep going" versus "stop or pivot"? Examples to weigh:

- Do I want to play another run?
- Can a friend play it without being told how?
- Does combat still feel good after 15 minutes?
- Does it hold frame rate on the Quest browser?

The answer becomes the success criteria section of the spec. It also limits scope: anything the slice doesn't need in order to test these signals is a candidate for cutting.
