# Oakvale's items

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05, 06

## Question

What does the inventory put into Oakvale, the starting zone?

- Each class's starting kit, and what's in the bag and on the belt when a new character first stands before Marshal Hale. Each class's weapon comes from the Abilities map.
- Each quest's reward: a pick of two or three items, with a weapon for each class at What Lies Below in place of Hale's longsword alone.
- The leader's orders as a quest item in the bag, and what happens to it at the hand-in.
- What Oakvale's camps and the Warden drop, and where its chests stand.
- The smith's and the innkeeper's stock in Oakvale.
- What an existing character's save becomes: the plain sword and Hale's longsword as items.

## Answer

Settled by Claude **on Tom's behalf** on 2026-09-30, taking the recommended option at every fork. The item names are placeholders, cheap to change; the ranger's and mage's weapon names wait on the Abilities map's "How the ranger fights" and "How the mage fights".

- **The starting kit** every new character wears, all white and item level 1:
  - A worn tunic and worn boots.
  - Their class's weapon and off hand: the warrior's plain sword and round shield (today's), the ranger's short bow and quiver, and the mage's apprentice's wand and glass focus (placeholders).
  - Three minor healing potions on the belt, stacked in the right hip's slot.
  - The bag starts empty, with 0 coins.
- **Each hand-in offers a pick**, laid out on Hale's board as items you carry into your bag. The pick fits your class: the attributes are your class's main one and Stamina, and the weapons are your class's.
  - **Raiders in the Fields:** a pick of two greens at item level 2, the Farmstead Gloves (hands) or the Hedgerow Boots (feet). XP as today.
  - **The Lumber Camp:** a pick of two greens at item level 3, the Timberline Leggings (legs) or the Marshal's Cap (head). The leader's orders, a quest item, are taken from you at the hand-in.
  - **What Lies Below:** a pick of two blues at item level 5, your class's weapon or a chest piece (the Warden's Mantle). The warrior's weapon is Hale's old longsword, darker-bladed with a gilded guard. The ranger gets Hale's old hunting bow and the mage a crypt-warded staff (placeholders). Hale's sword leaves their hip only when a warrior takes it.
- **The leader's orders** go on the bag's quest page when touched in the tent, as today's pickup does, and are gone at the hand-in.
- **What drops:** the [Loot](05-loot.md) rule at each camp's level. That is level 1 at the farm, 2 at the lumber camp and the watchtower, 3 near the mine's mouth and 4 deeper in. The leader and the deep brutes always drop a green or a blue, and the Warden a blue and a green each time it's beaten.
- **Oakvale's three chests**, each opening once per character:
  - At the top of the watchtower (level 2).
  - In the lumber camp leader's tent, beside the orders (level 2).
  - The strongbox of ore at the bandits' dig in the mine (level 4), which today is a prop.
- **The smith's stock in Oakvale:**
  - Each class's white weapon and off hand at item levels 1, 3 and 5.
  - A white armour piece for every slot at item levels 2 and 4.
  - The innkeeper sells the minor healing potion. Oakvale's stash chest stands by the Golden Tankard's hearth.
- **An existing save** becomes a warrior (the Abilities map's call). It wears the starting kit, with today's `sword` deciding the main hand: the plain sword, or Hale's old longsword as the blue item-level-5 weapon if they already handed in What Lies Below. It also gets three minor healing potions on the belt and 0 coins. A character who already handed quests in gets no retroactive picks.
