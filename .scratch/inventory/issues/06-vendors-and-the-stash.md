# Vendors and the stash

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

How do you buy, sell and store?

- Talking to a vendor: the talk board with buy and sell buttons, or the bag panel opened beside them, or laying items on the counter by hand.
- Coin prices for Professions' materials and crafted goods, which vendors buy; the innkeeper sells only the minor healing potion.
- What the smith and the innkeeper stock, whether stock runs out, and whether you can buy back what you sold by mistake.
- Selling junk: one by one, or all at once.
- The stash in a chest at the inn: how many slots, how it opens, and whether other zones get one.
