# Vendors and the stash

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

How do you buy, sell and store?

- Talking to a vendor: the talk board with buy and sell buttons, or the bag panel opened beside them, or laying items on the counter by hand.
- Coin prices for Professions' materials and crafted goods, which vendors buy; the innkeeper sells only the minor healing potion.
- What the smith and the innkeeper stock, whether stock runs out, and whether you can buy back what you sold by mistake.
- Selling junk: one by one, or all at once.
- The stash in a chest at the inn: how many slots, how it opens, and whether other zones get one.

## Answer

Settled by Claude **on Tom's behalf** on 2026-09-30, taking the recommended option at every fork and building on [The bag and the gear panel](03-the-bag-and-the-gear-panel.md)'s pick (touch, hold the grip, carry). Every number is a starting point.

- **Trading is carrying.** Walk up to a vendor, within the talk board's 2.3 m and facing them, and their **wares board** unfolds beside them where Hale's talk board would. Your bag panel opens beside it on its own, with no reach needed. Both use the bag's touch-and-carry.
  - To buy, carry an item from the wares board into a bag slot. It costs its price the moment you let go.
  - To sell, carry an item from your bag onto the wares board. The coins come at once.
  - An item's card shows its price, the buy price on the wares board and the sell price in your bag.
  - What you can't afford sits dimmed on the board and refuses with the red slot and strong buzz, as a locked gear slot does.
  - Walk off 3.6 m and both boards fold. The world doesn't pause.
- **Stock never runs out.** A vendor always has their list.
- **Buyback:** the last six things you sold wait in a "Sold" row along the wares board's bottom, at the price you got, until you leave the zone.
- **Selling junk:** a "Sell junk" button on the wares board sells every grey in the bag at once, pressed with a fist or the weapon's tip like the talk board's buttons.
- **Who sells what:**
  - **The smith** buys anything and sells white gear: each class's weapon and off hand, and armour for every slot, at the zone's levels. The level lock applies: an item above your level can be bought but not worn.
  - **The innkeeper** sells the minor healing potion.
  - Crafted consumables are crafted only (the Professions map).
- **Prices:** the rule from [What an item is](02-what-an-item-is.md): an item sells for item level × 2 coins (grey), × 3 (white), × 8 (green) or × 20 (blue), and vendors sell at 4 times that. Fixed prices for the rest, as the vendor buys them, with the vendor's price 4 times that:
  - Minor healing potion: 2 coins (8 to buy at the inn).
  - Rage draught, minor mana potion, elixir of the keen eye and whetstone: 3 coins each.
  - Copper ore, rough stone, Hearthleaf and Duskcap: 1 coin each.
  - Copper bar: 3 coins.
  - The checked sum: at level 3 an ordinary kill averages about 6 coins plus about 2 in junk, so a white level-3 chest piece (36 coins) costs about five kills and a potion one. Oakvale's whole route pays roughly 300 to 400 coins.
- **Quest items** can't be sold, stashed or dropped.
- **The stash:** a chest by the Golden Tankard's hearth. Touch its lid and the **stash panel** opens beside your bag panel: 32 slots in two pages of 16, carried to and from as in the bag. Every zone's inn gets such a chest, and they all open the same stash, as a WoW bank does.
