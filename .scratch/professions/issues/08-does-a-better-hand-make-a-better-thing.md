# Does a better hand make a better thing

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05, 06, 07

## Question

Should doing the motion well change what you get, and if so, how?

- **What could change:** more ore or herbs from a spot, a faster make, a chance of a better rarity, or nothing (the motion is only for feel).
- **Fits with:** Inventory's items are hand-made with fixed stats, so a better result can't be a random roll on stats; crafted gear is never better than drops; the activity should stay calm.
- **Failure:** can a botched strike or brew waste materials, or only take longer?

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option, which all three prototypes already built toward.

- **A better hand buys speed, never a better or a different thing.** A glint strike breaks a vein in 2 swings instead of 5 ([Swinging the pick and cutting herbs](05-swinging-the-pick-and-cutting-herbs.md)); a great strike works an anvil mark at once instead of in two ([Hammering at the anvil](06-hammering-at-the-anvil.md)); a quick, tidy grind and stir finish a brew sooner ([Brewing at the alchemy table](07-brewing-at-the-alchemy-table.md)). What comes out is always the same: the same count of ore and herbs from a spot, and the same item from a recipe.
- **No yield or rarity bonus.** Inventory's items have fixed stats, crafted gear is never better than drops, and a bonus would turn a calm activity into a chase for perfect swings.
- **Nothing fails and nothing is wasted.** A tap, a strike off the glint, cold metal or a slow stir only takes longer. Materials leave the bag only when a make starts (on the anvil's board, or as herbs drop into the mortar), and walking off mid-work keeps the work where it stands. A herb cut through the leaves instead of the stems trims one leaf and says "cut lower"; it's never lost.
- **Proficiency counts things, not swings** ([How a profession grows](04-how-a-profession-grows.md)), so a clumsy hand grows as fast per item as a deft one.
