## Question

Should doing the motion well change what you get, and if so, how?

- **Fits with:** Inventory's items are hand-made with fixed stats, so a better result can't be a random roll on stats; crafted gear is never better than drops; the activity should stay calm.
- **Failure:** can a botched strike or brew waste materials, or only take longer?
