# Trainers and first lessons

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 04

## Question

Who teaches each profession in Oakvale, where, and what is each intro quest?

- **Trainers:** the smith already works the anvil and can teach Mining and Smithing. Who teaches Herbalism and Alchemy: the farmer, the innkeeper, or someone new (and where they stand), and where the alchemy table is (the inn, the house by the well, a new spot).
- **Intro quests:** a short one per profession that teaches the motion (take three ore from the vein by the smithy, make your first bar), how it's offered (the talk board, markers over the trainer), and whether it sits beside Marshal Hale's chain or depends on it.
- **Buying recipes:** how the trainer shows what's for sale at your tier, and in which UI (the talk board's first pass, before Tom's planned UI overhaul).
- **Barks:** what the trainers say as you pass once you've learned their craft.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**: Tom asked for the rest of the map to be worked without him, taking the recommended option each time. Every number is a starting point, to tune on the headset, and every name is a placeholder.


- **The smith teaches Mining and Smithing** at the anvil in the smithy, where they already work. They stop being a villager and become a **trainer**: a gold "!" over them when they have a quest, the same talk board as Marshal Hale.
- **A new herbalist teaches Herbalism and Alchemy** in the house by the well, which opens today but stands empty. The **alchemy table** stands against its back wall: a mortar, a small burner, flasks and hanging bundles of herbs. The herbalist is a friendly character in the one human body, dressed in undyed linen with a green-stained apron and a herb satchel, grey-haired, standing at the table. A human costs 630 to 900 triangles, and the house's inside is hidden from the outdoors, so the budget barely moves.
- **One intro quest per pair**, each teaching the motion in context:
  - **The smith's "Ore and Fire":** accepting teaches Mining and Smithing and hangs the pick on your belt. Objectives: break 2 copper veins (the nearest are on the hill behind the smithy), then make a whetstone at the anvil. The hand-in pays XP and a few coins, and the whetstone is yours.
  - **The herbalist's "Leaves for the Pot":** accepting teaches Herbalism and Alchemy and hangs the knife on your belt. Objectives: gather 4 Hearthleaf in the fields, then brew a minor healing potion at the table. The hand-in pays XP and a few coins, and the potion is yours.
  - Each teaches its grade's first recipes (the copper bar and the whetstone; the minor healing potion). The rest are bought.
- **They open after Raiders in the Fields** is handed in, so the first minutes stay Marshal Hale's. They run alongside Hale's chain, never blocking it.
- **More than one quest at once:** Hale's spec allowed one active quest. Now up to three can be active (Hale's and the two intro quests). The tracker lists each active quest with its lines, newest at the bottom, and the quest arrow sits beside the first unfinished objective of the quest you took most recently.
- **Buying recipes:** the trainer's board gets a "Train" button beside "Accept" and "Goodbye", showing the recipes of your grade with their price in coins and the proficiency each needs, in grey until you have it. Press one to buy it. It stays the talk board's plain first pass until Tom's UI overhaul.
- **Barks:** the smith keeps their hammering lines and adds one once you've learned ("Keep your pick sharp and your fire hot."). The herbalist: "Mind where you step, there's Hearthleaf by that road." and, once you've learned, "Bring me Duskcap from the old mine and I'll show you something stronger."
