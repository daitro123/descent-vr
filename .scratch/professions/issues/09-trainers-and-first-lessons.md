# Trainers and first lessons

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 04

## Question

Who teaches each profession in Oakvale, where, and what is each intro quest?

- **Trainers:** the smith already works the anvil and can teach Mining and Smithing. Who teaches Herbalism and Alchemy: the farmer, the innkeeper, or someone new (and where they stand), and where the alchemy table is (the inn, the house by the well, a new spot).
- **Intro quests:** a short one per profession that teaches the motion (take three ore from the vein by the smithy, make your first bar), how it's offered (the talk board, markers over the trainer), and whether it sits beside Marshal Hale's chain or depends on it.
- **Buying recipes:** how the trainer shows what's for sale at your tier, and in which UI (the talk board's first pass, before Tom's planned UI overhaul).
- **Barks:** what the trainers say as you pass once you've learned their craft.
