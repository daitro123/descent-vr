## What was built

Built on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option at every fork. Nobody has drawn the bow on a headset yet: every number is the prototype's first guess, in `CONFIG.ranger` and `CONFIG.classes.ranger`.

- **The ranger is playable:** `PLAYABLE` in `classes.ts` is the warrior and the ranger, so the ranger's card shows on the new-character form and `newRecord` starts it in its kit (a short bow and a quiver). `?arena&class=ranger` is the level-1 ranger with every base ability to 10.
- **One bar per class on the player** (`src/player/player.ts`): `player.resource` holds what your class's bar holds, and `player.stats.resource` says which bar it is (rage, focus or mana), its size, where it starts and what it gains a second, in a fight (`player.fighting`) or out. Rage drains 2 a second as before, focus refills 10 a second always, and mana (ticket 23) would refill by its own two rates. `reset` puts it where it starts (no rage, full focus). `player.rage` is still the warrior's word for it (0 for another class, and setting it does nothing then), so the warrior's code and every check reading rage is unchanged. `Combat.use` checks and spends `player.resource`; the belt reads it too.
- **`player.class`**: the Player takes its class (`new Player(camera, renderer, ground, klass)`); the Adventure passes the state's, the arena the flag's. Only the warrior's hands get the sword and shield.
- **The bow** (`src/player/bow.ts`) is the prototype's `RangerBow`, its numbers in `CONFIG.ranger.bow`: in the left hand, the limb along the fist, the string coming back towards you; nock by touching the string's middle half with the trigger held, pull back, let go; a draw under 15% puts the arrow away. The nocked arrow glows while it's a Power Shot.
- **The kit in a fight** (`src/combat/ranger.ts`, `RangerKit`, which Combat makes for a ranger and lands the blows of): the draw and its haptics (a tick every 50 ms growing with the bend, a click at full draw, a pulse on release: the prototype's), unlimited arrows, the ward on the bow hand's grip, Power Shot and Snare Trap. It stands aside while the bag is open or your hands are the bench's (`held`), and puts the bow away with an empty main hand (`worn`, set from the gear in `dressHands`) or a station's tools in hand.
- **The arrows** (`src/combat/shots.ts`, `Shots`): one instanced mesh; damage 6 to 30 (times your damage) and speed 14 to 42 m/s in step with the draw (`shotOf`), gravity, a head hit at the enemy's multiplier and an exposed one ×1.5 (`arrowDamage`), a raised guard stops it, an enemy walking home "Evade"s it. It hits the nearest enemy along its flight each frame; one that still pierces goes on to the next, never the same one twice.
- **The ward** (`src/combat/ward.ts`, `Ward`): a disc 0.16 m past the bow hand, up to 1.2 s while squeezed, back 2 s after; it stops enemy arrows and in its first 0.35 s sends them back through Combat's reflect (30 and the archer exposed). Combat asks it first, before the body.
- **Power Shot** (A/X while drawing, level 2, 20 focus, 4 s): `Combat.primary(enemies)` is now what A/X does in both games: the warrior's War Cry, or for a ranger with an arrow nocked `Combat.use('powerShot')`. The arrow glows gold-white; loosed, it deals double and passes through the first enemy it hits. Pressing it again on the same arrow, or with no arrow drawn, does nothing and spends nothing. Its name and cost (or "not enough focus", "ready in N s") float over the bow.
- **Snare Trap** (ring, level 3, 20 focus, 10 s; `src/combat/traps.ts`, `Traps`): an open iron trap at your feet (one instanced mesh, 204 triangles each, at most three lying about; a fourth ends the oldest). It lies 30 s, and the first enemy a blow could land on whose body reaches over it springs it: `enemy.afflict('rooted', 4)` (brutes 2 s, the Warden not at all, "immune"), which does no damage and doesn't pull its camp. "ROOTED" floats over it, and ticket 20's vines grow at its feet.
- **Gestures stand aside while you draw:** Combat's `busy` (an arrow nocked) is the Adventure's and the arena's `GestureHost.busy`, so a stroke with an arrow on the string is dropped.
- **Colours and sounds:** `ABILITY_COLOUR.powerShot` (gold-white) and `.snareTrap` (leaf green); `sfx.powerShot`, `sfx.snareTrap` and `sfx.trapSnap`.
- **The belt's right orb** is your class's bar: rage as before (once the War Cry comes), focus in gold from level 1, mana in blue (for ticket 23). Pips read `player.resource`.
- **The prototype moved aside:** `&class=ranger` plays the built ranger, and `&variant=` does nothing there. The prototype stays in the repo for Tom to compare, at `?arena&class=ranger-prototype&variant=ward|knife|kite`, over the warrior's arena as before. `&gestures` still runs the gesture prototype over the ranger's prototype (`&class=ranger&gestures`), as it was built, until ticket 27.

**Tests:** `tests/ranger.test.ts` (19) at the combat seam: a real `RangerKit` with its bow in hands posed by code, loosing at real enemies. The draw's damage and speed (30 at 42 m/s at full draw, 18 at 28 at half, a short draw put away, arrows never running out, times your damage); two full-draw body shots killing a grunt; Power Shot only with an arrow on the string and once an arrow, passing through the first grunt of two in a line to kill both at double damage, but only through one of three; the ward's hold and cooldown, sending an arrow back while fresh and stopping it after, and meeting an enemy's arrow before your body; Snare Trap rooting the grunt that walks onto it for 4 s unhurt and standing still, brutes half and the Warden not at all, lying 30 s, never springing for one still rising, a fourth ending the oldest; focus's numbers.

**Headless check:** `.scratch/abilities/checks/ranger-class.mjs` (CHECK_RESULT). In the arena as a ranger: the bow and no sword or shield, every base ability, full focus; two full-draw shots kill wave 1's grunt and a short draw does less; A with nothing drawn does nothing; A while drawing makes a Power Shot for 20 focus (not twice) that kills two grunts in a line; a ring drawn with an arrow nocked is dropped, and drawn free lays the trap for 20 focus, which roots a grunt walking onto it where it stands, unhurt; the ward sends an archer's arrow back; `&variant=knife` is the ranger and `&class=ranger-prototype` the prototype.
