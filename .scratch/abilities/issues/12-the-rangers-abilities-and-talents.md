# The ranger's abilities and talents

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 07, 08, 09, 10

## Question

What are the ranger's base abilities from level 1 to 10, and what's in its two talent trees?

- Which base ability each level brings, what it does, what it costs and how it's used (by gesture or button, per [Using abilities by gesture](07-using-abilities-by-gesture.md)).
- Two trees: for example Marksmanship and Survival (traps and knives). No pets: four-legged creatures need a new rig.
- Each tree's talents to the depth a level-10 character can reach, and a sketch of its deepest talents to level 20.
- Which enemies and places in Oakvale each ability is meant to answer.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option at every fork. Damage is in level-1 terms and grows with your damage multiplier like every blow; costs follow [Rage, focus and mana](09-rage-focus-and-mana.md) and never grow with level. The numbers are starting points, to tune on the headset.

**How abilities are used** (per [Using abilities by gesture](07-using-abilities-by-gesture.md)): each base gesture ability has its own shape, listed below; a tier-3 talent ability takes the triangle, and anything later the next free shape. The talent page can swap which shape holds which. Hold the right grip, draw, let go.

**What stays as it is:** the bow (nocked by touching the string, damage by the draw, arrows that never run out), the ward on the bow hand's grip (free on its cooldown) and the dash on B/Y. Focus starts full and refills 10 a second. A gesture can't be drawn with an arrow nocked, so gestures come between shots; the one button ability works mid-draw.

**Base abilities to level 10:**

| Level | Ability | Used by | Cost | Cooldown | What it does | Meant for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Power Shot | A/X while drawing | 20 focus | 4 s | The nocked arrow glows: double damage, and it passes through the first enemy to hit one behind. | The farm's and lumber camp's thugs in a line. |
| 3 | Snare Trap | gesture (ring) | 20 focus | 10 s | A trap drops at your feet; the first enemy to step on it within 30 s is rooted for 4 s. | A grunt closing in: drop it, dash back, shoot. |
| 6 | Volley | gesture (Z) | 35 focus | 12 s | Your next arrow splits into five in a 20° fan, each at 60%. | A camp of five. |
| 8 | Scatter | gesture (V) | 25 focus | 15 s | A gust from your hand knocks back and staggers every enemy within 3 m in front of you. | The close-range gap: what to do when the trap and the dash are spent. |
| 10 | Hunter's Mark | gesture (S) | 20 focus | 1 s | The enemy you face is marked for 20 s: it takes 15% more from you and its outline shows through walls. | Archers behind cover, the Warden. |

**Sketched past 10:** Explosive Arrow at 14 (35 focus: the next arrow bursts for 20 within 2 m) and Rapid Fire at 18 (40 focus: for 6 s a half draw hits as hard as a full one).

## Comments

**2026-09-30, on Tom's behalf:**

- Power Shot on the button: it's the one ability a ranger wants mid-draw, when both hands are busy and no gesture can be drawn. Everything else waits for the gap between shots, as the gesture prototype found.
- Snare Trap first: the prototype left the ranger weak up close on purpose, and the first gesture a ranger learns fills that gap cheaply.
- Scatter, not a kick or a knife: the ranger prototype turned the knife down (it's a second melee class), and a kick needs feet the game can't see.
- No pets: four-legged creatures need a new rig, and a bird is a new rig too.
