# Bows, spells and abilities in shipped VR games

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

How do shipped VR games make a bow, spellcasting and triggered abilities feel good, and what do they avoid?

- **Bows:** how the draw, nock and release work with two tracked controllers (which hand holds the bow, how an arrow is taken, aim assist, haptics), and how archers defend themselves without a shield. Look at In Death, Elven Assassin, Skyrim VR, Asgard's Wrath and The Elder Scrolls: Blades VR or similar.
- **Spellcasting:** how spells are chosen and cast (gestures, drawn runes, hand poses, buttons, a wheel), and what the off hand does. Look at The Wizards, Blade & Sorcery, Skyrim VR, Waltz of the Wizard and Asgard's Wrath.
- **Abilities:** how games with many abilities (Asgard's Wrath 2, Hellsweeper, Blade & Sorcery) fit them onto a Quest controller, and how many a player can hold in their head in a fight.
- **Comfort and fatigue:** what the games learned about arm fatigue, missed gestures and sickness.

Findings go in `.scratch/abilities/research/bows-spells-and-abilities-in-shipped-vr-games.md`, each claim with its source (developer talks, official docs and patch notes, first-hand reviews that show the mechanic).

## Answer

Resolved by a research agent on 2026-09-30. The findings are in [bows-spells-and-abilities-in-shipped-vr-games.md](../research/bows-spells-and-abilities-in-shipped-vr-games.md). The proxy blocked the agent from opening pages, so every claim rests on search-result extracts and is marked medium or low confidence. The Elder Scrolls: Blades, The Wizards' off hand and missed gestures, Elven Assassin's spells and the Until You Fall GDC talk weren't covered.

- **Bow:** held in the left hand, drawn with the right. An arrow is nocked by touching the string and holding the trigger, with no reach to a quiver (In Death, Asgard's Wrath 2). Damage scales with the draw, haptics build through it and pulse on release, and the bow hand's grip raises a short ward against projectiles.
- **Casting:** a spell in each hand, charged by holding the trigger and cast by throwing or pushing the palm out. How you throw can shape the spell. Both hands brought together make a merged spell, a fit for the deepest talents. No spell wheel mid-fight.
- **Abilities:** grip held, then a flick up, down, in or out calls one of about four per hand (Hellsweeper). Every recognised gesture gets a sound, a flash and a buzz; thresholds are loose and adjustable, since strict gestures are the main complaint.
- **Fatigue:** poses at chest height with the elbows low, rests built into fights (rage, focus and mana can pace them), and a seated mode.
- **For Inventory:** the research advises against reaching into a quiver to take an arrow, while Inventory's map names the ranger's off-hand slot a quiver. The slot can stay as gear without a reach; [How the ranger fights](05-how-the-ranger-fights.md) settles it.
