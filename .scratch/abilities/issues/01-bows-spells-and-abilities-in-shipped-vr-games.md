# Bows, spells and abilities in shipped VR games

Type: research
Status: claimed
Blocked by:

## Question

How do shipped VR games make a bow, spellcasting and triggered abilities feel good, and what do they avoid?

- **Bows:** how the draw, nock and release work with two tracked controllers (which hand holds the bow, how an arrow is taken, aim assist, haptics), and how archers defend themselves without a shield. Look at In Death, Elven Assassin, Skyrim VR, Asgard's Wrath and The Elder Scrolls: Blades VR or similar.
- **Spellcasting:** how spells are chosen and cast (gestures, drawn runes, hand poses, buttons, a wheel), and what the off hand does. Look at The Wizards, Blade & Sorcery, Skyrim VR, Waltz of the Wizard and Asgard's Wrath.
- **Abilities:** how games with many abilities (Asgard's Wrath 2, Hellsweeper, Blade & Sorcery) fit them onto a Quest controller, and how many a player can hold in their head in a fight.
- **Comfort and fatigue:** what the games learned about arm fatigue, missed gestures and sickness.

Findings go in `.scratch/abilities/research/bows-spells-and-abilities-in-shipped-vr-games.md`, each claim with its source (developer talks, official docs and patch notes, first-hand reviews that show the mechanic).
