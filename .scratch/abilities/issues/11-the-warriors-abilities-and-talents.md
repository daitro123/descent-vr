# The warrior's abilities and talents

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 07, 08, 09, 10

## Question

What are the warrior's base abilities from level 1 to 10, and what's in its two talent trees?

- Which base ability each level brings, what it does, what it costs and how it's used (by gesture or button, per [Using abilities by gesture](07-using-abilities-by-gesture.md)).
- Today's War Cry (level 2), Earthshaker (level 3), dash and shield bash: which stay base abilities, which become talents, and what changes.
- Two trees: Arms and Protection are the working names.
- Each tree's talents to the depth a level-10 character can reach, and a sketch of its deepest talents to level 20.
- Which enemies and places in Oakvale each ability is meant to answer.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option at every fork. Damage is in level-1 terms and grows with your damage multiplier like every blow; costs follow [Rage, focus and mana](09-rage-focus-and-mana.md) and never grow with level. The numbers are starting points, to tune on the headset.

**How abilities are used** (per [Using abilities by gesture](07-using-abilities-by-gesture.md)): each base gesture ability has its own shape, listed below; a tier-3 talent ability takes the triangle, and anything later the next free shape. The talent page can swap which shape holds which. Hold the right grip, draw, let go.

**What stays as it is:** the sword, the shield, the shield bash (6 damage, knockback, exposes a wind-up) and the dash on B/Y are the warrior's plain kit, with no cost. Rage builds and drains as today.

**Base abilities to level 10:**

| Level | Ability | Used by | Cost | Cooldown | What it does | Meant for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | War Cry | A/X | 50 rage | none | As today: 15 damage, knockback and stagger within 3.5 m, then 8 s of frenzy (+35% damage). | The lumber camp's five closing in at once. |
| 3 | Earthshaker | the sword's tip driven into the ground (a rule check, no grip) | 35 rage | 0.8 s | As today: 30 damage and knockback within 2.2 m of where the tip lands. | The mine's crowds. |
| 6 | Heroic Throw | gesture (ring) | 15 rage | 6 s | A spectral axe leaves your hand along the gesture's end and flies up to 20 m to the enemy you face: 20 damage and a stagger. | Archers you can't reach: the watchtower lookout, the mine's skeleton archers. |
| 8 | Shield Wall | gesture (Z) | 25 rage | 30 s | For 6 s a block takes no damage from any blow, the brute's heavy swing included, and doesn't numb the arm. The slam stays unblockable. | The brutes in the mine's deep rooms; the Warden's heavy blows. |
| 10 | Sweeping Strikes | gesture (V) | 30 rage | 20 s | For 8 s every sword hit also strikes the nearest other enemy within 1.5 m of the one you hit, for 60%. | Camps fought in a knot. |

**Sketched past 10:** Execute at 14 (20 rage: your next sword hit within 3 s kills an enemy under 20% health, bosses excepted) and Rallying Cry at 18 (40 rage: heal 30% of your health over 10 s).

## Comments

**2026-09-30, on Tom's behalf:**

- Everything today's warrior has stays base, so Oakvale's levels 2 and 3 play exactly as built.
- Heroic Throw first: the warrior's only real gap is range, and the first gesture a warrior learns should be cheap (a ring cast by mistake costs 15).
- Shield Wall doesn't block the brute's slam: dodging the slam is what the brute tests (Oakvale's enemies ticket).
- Arms and Protection keep the warrior's two halves, the sword and the shield, as its two trees; each tier-3 ability changes the next blow of that half, so it's used with a swing or a bash rather than as a separate move.
- No charge: moving the player fast and far on their own is the thing VR comfort warns against most; the dash is enough.
