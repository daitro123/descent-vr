# Talent tree rules

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 04

## Question

What are the rules every class's talent tree follows?

- Two trees per class and a point every level from 2 (decided in charting). How many rows, how many points to open each row, and how many points a talent can take.
- Whether you can spend points in both trees, and how deep one tree can go by level 10 and by level 20.
- Which talents grant abilities (the deepest ones, decided in charting) and which change numbers or how an ability works.
- Undoing picks: never, free anywhere, or at a place in the world, and whether it costs anything.
- When you pick: the moment you level, or any time later out of a fight.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option at every fork; the reasons are under Comments.

- **Each class has two trees of five tiers.** A tier opens once you've spent 3 points in that tree for each tier above it: tier 2 at 3 points, 3 at 6, 4 at 9, 5 at 12.
- **Each tier holds two or three talents,** about twelve a tree. A talent that changes numbers or how an ability works takes 1 to 3 points; a talent that grants an ability takes 1.
- **Two ability talents per tree:** one in tier 3 and the capstone alone in tier 5. With every point in one tree, tier 3 comes at level 8 and the capstone at 14. By level 10 a character can take tier 3 of one tree; tier 4 opens at 11 and the capstone at 14, so this map sketches them only.
- **Points go anywhere open,** in both trees if you like. Hybrids are allowed; depth is the reward for staying in one tree.
- **A level-up gives a point to spend when you choose,** out of a fight. Unspent points wait, and the level-up's lines say "Talent point: open your talents".
- **Talents are a page of the panel the Inventory map opens** (reach over your shoulder and squeeze the grip), beside the bag and gear. No new gesture or button. How the page looks waits on Tom's UI overhaul; a plain first pass shows both trees side by side, each talent as a button with its points.
- **Resetting is free and happens anywhere out of a fight:** one button returns every point. There's no per-talent undo and no cost.
- **Talents that grant abilities fill the next free way to use one** (a flick or a button, per [Using abilities by gesture](07-using-abilities-by-gesture.md)); a reset takes the ability back.

## Comments

**2026-09-30, on Tom's behalf:**

- Five tiers of 3 points: 19 points by level 20 in two trees of about 12 talents each make a full tree unreachable, so every character is a choice. WoW's 5 points a tier would put the capstone out of reach below level 20.
- Abilities in tier 3 and tier 5: charting said the deepest talents grant abilities; one halfway gives a talent ability inside the levels this map specs in detail.
- Free reset: single-player and no trainer, so a cost would only punish trying things. Out of a fight, so it can't be a mid-fight swap.
- On the Inventory panel: one gesture opens every page about your character, and Tom's UI overhaul can redo them together.
