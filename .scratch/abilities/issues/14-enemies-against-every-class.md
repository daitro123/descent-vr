# Enemies against every class

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 05, 06, 11, 12, 13

## Question

What do Oakvale's enemies need now that a character can fight from 20 m away, root, freeze and slow them?

- Pulls from range: the 8 m notice radius and the rule that a hurt enemy fights.
- The 30 m leash against a kiting ranger or a blinking mage.
- How roots, freezes, slows and knockbacks land on each behaviour and on the Warden.
- Whether "two enemies mid-attack at once" (the arena) and "three may swing at once" (camps) still hold.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-09-30 **by Claude on Tom's behalf**, taking the recommended option at every fork.

- **No new behaviours.** Grunts, archers, brutes and the Warden stay as built; the enemy archers are already the pressure on anyone who stands back.
- **Pulls from range work as today:** hurting an enemy from any distance pulls it and whoever of its camp stands within 10 m of it. A ranger opening from 20 m gets the same camp a warrior gets at 8 m, only further away.
- **The leash stays 30 m from the enemy's post,** not from you. Kiting in circles near a camp works; dragging a camp across the zone turns it home as today. A frozen or rooted enemy's leash clock still runs.
- **Three new states on every enemy:** *rooted* (can't move, can still strike what's in reach), *frozen* (can't act at all until it ends or a hit breaks it) and *slowed* (moves and winds up that much slower). A knockback or stagger from an ability works as the War Cry's does today.
- **Brutes shrug off half:** roots and freezes last half as long on a brute and slows are half as strong. **The Warden ignores roots and freezes,** and slows on it are halved, so its fight stays a boss fight.
- **The limits on attackers stay:** two mid-attack at once in the arena and three in a camp. A frozen enemy doesn't count.
- **Kills pay whoever the class.** Nothing about XP or camps refilling changes.
- **To check on the headset:** whether a ranger clears the lumber camp's five at level 2 to 3 with Power Shot and a trap, and whether a mage survives the mine's brutes with Frost Nova and the blink. The camps' strength (×1.4) is the one number to lower for a class that struggles.
