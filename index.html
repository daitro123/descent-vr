DESCENT VR — white box

Warrior combat prototype. Put on a headset and press Enter VR.
  • Right hand: sword — swing fast (blade glows blue) to deal damage. Head hits crit.
  • Left hand: shield — put it between you and the club to block; push into the blow to parry.
  • Enemies glow orange while winding up: hit them first to interrupt, block, or step back.
  • Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn.
  • A / X: War Cry (costs 50 rage — the right belt orb).
  • Grab or walk over red orbs to heal.
