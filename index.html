DESCENT VR — Oakvale

Oakvale, a forest valley and the first zone of a bigger world. You start at the crossroads with sword and shield, facing Marshal Hale, who has work for you. The game saves itself in this browser, so you carry on where you stood. Put on a headset and press Enter VR.
  • Left stick: walk, where you look. Right stick: snap turn. B / Y: dash.
  • Right hand: sword. Left hand: shield.
  • Look down at your belt for your health.

DESCENT VR — the arena

Warrior versus the dead in the crypt hall: seven waves and the Bone Warden. Put on a headset and press Enter VR.
  • Right hand: sword. Swing fast (the blade glows blue) to deal damage. Head hits crit.
  • Left hand: shield. Hold it where the blow will land; push it into the blow to parry. Punch enemies with it to bash.
  • Orange glow: blockable. Red glow: unblockable, get out of the way. Duck horizontal slashes.
  • Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn. B / Y: dash.
  • A / X: War Cry (50 rage). Sword tip into the floor: Earthshaker (35 rage).
  • Touch red orbs to heal.
