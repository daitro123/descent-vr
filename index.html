DESCENT VR — combat prototype

Warrior versus the crypt: seven waves and the Bone Warden. Put on a headset and press Enter VR.
  • Right hand: sword. Swing fast (the blade glows blue) to deal damage. Head hits crit.
  • Left hand: shield. Hold it where the blow will land; push it into the blow to parry. Punch enemies with it to bash.
  • Orange glow: blockable. Red glow: unblockable, get out of the way. Duck horizontal slashes.
  • Left stick: move. Right stick: snap turn. B / Y: dash.
  • A / X: War Cry (50 rage). Sword tip into the floor: Earthshaker (35 rage).
  • Touch red orbs to heal.
Model inspector: every enemy and its animations on a turntable.
Map viewer: fly freely through the maps.
